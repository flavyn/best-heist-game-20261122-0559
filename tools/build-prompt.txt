Game name: "Vault Shift" — an original top-down heist puzzle. A thief crosses three rooms (Night Vault, Neon Exchange, Freight Hold) to steal a glowing loot target and reach the extraction gate without being caught. Each room shows, in ONE frame: the room floor, the thief, guards with translucent VISION CONES, sweeping CCTV cameras with their own cones, solid crates/desks as COVER, the gold loot target, and a large ALARM METER. Guards patrol fixed loops. Cameras sweep on a sine angle. Line of sight is blocked by cover: standing behind a crate makes the thief unseen; pressing Space while adjacent to cover crouches behind it (drawn smaller, dimmer, with a "HIDDEN" tag). Tools at a cost: press 1 to spend 2 credits on a decoy coin that distracts the nearest guard for 3 seconds; press 2 to spend 3 credits on a camera jammer that blinds cameras for 4 seconds. Credits are earned by picking up loot and are shown in the HUD. Detection fills while the thief is in any unblocked cone; full detection triggers a fair caught state with a quick reset of the room, not an instant unwinnable game. Reaching extraction after collecting the room's loot clears the room and pays out credits. Deterministic: seeded patrols, no Math.random at runtime. Three rooms with different layouts, guard counts and camera placements, increasing difficulty.

Controls: WASD / arrows move, mouse click on a floor tile sets a waypoint the thief walks toward (clicks on cover or guards are ignored), Space toggles cover-hide, 1 decoy, 2 jammer, R retry room, N advance when cleared. Audio: short WebAudio blips made in code, no sound files.
